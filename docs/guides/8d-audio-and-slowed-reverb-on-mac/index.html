8D audio and slowed + reverb for any app on Mac

Updated 16 November 2026 · by notshevvv, maker of CallGuard

To get 8D audio or slowed + reverb for any app on a Mac, use a system-wide audio app. CallGuard is free and works live on Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music or anything else: turn on 8D Audio to make the music circle your head, or pick the Slowed + Reverb preset in Effects.

What 8D audio actually is

"8D" songs move the music slowly around your head. CallGuard's 8D Audio does it live, using the same cues your brain uses to place a sound. It's louder in the near ear, arrives a fraction of a millisecond later in the far ear (up to 0.6 ms), and sounds duller behind your head. One turn takes 5 to 18 seconds, set with a Speed slider. Use earphones: on speakers it only sounds like panning.

Slowed + reverb, live

Slowed + reverb edits lower a song's pitch and add a big room around it. Live audio can't actually play slower, because it would fall behind the video. So CallGuard's Slowed + Reverb preset lowers the pitch by 3 semitones and adds reverb, and the timing stays in sync. Other presets: Sped Up (pitch up), Ambience, Harmony (adds a soft octave layer) and Dreamy. You can fine-tune pitch, reverb, room size, harmony and stereo width.

How to turn it on

  1. Download CallGuard and open it from the menu bar.
  2. For 8D: switch on the 8D Audio card and pick a speed.
  3. For slowed + reverb: tap Slowed + Reverb in the Effects card.
  4. Allow "System Audio Recording" the first time. Nothing is recorded or saved.

Make small earbuds sound bigger

Studio Master adds the overtones of each bass note, so earbuds and laptop speakers let you hear deep bass they can't physically play. It also tightens the low end, dips boxiness at 320 Hz and adds air to the highs. A limiter keeps everything below −1 dBFS, so nothing clips.

On a call, all of these effects change only your music and videos. The other person's voice stays clean and centred.

Quick answers

Does it work with Spotify and YouTube?

Yes. CallGuard processes all Mac audio, so it works with any app that plays sound: Spotify, YouTube in any browser, Apple Music and more.

Does slowed + reverb change the song's speed?

No, live audio can't be slowed without falling behind. It lowers the pitch and adds reverb, so it sounds slowed while staying in sync.

Will effects change the voice on my call?

No. Effects, 8D and Studio Master apply to music and videos only. The call voice is left untouched.
