Why Bluetooth earbuds sound muffled on Mac calls, and how to fix it

Updated 16 November 2026 · by notshevvv, maker of CallGuard

Earbuds sound muffled on calls because using their microphone switches Bluetooth into headset mode, which carries sound at low quality (16 kHz, mono) in both directions. To keep stereo hi-fi, make the Mac's built-in microphone the input for calls. CallGuard's Hi-Fi Buds does that automatically.

What's happening

Most Bluetooth earbuds have two modes. Music mode (A2DP) plays rich stereo sound, but only in one direction. Headset mode (HFP) also carries the earbud microphone, so it squeezes everything into a narrow channel. On the earbuds we tested, that meant 16 kHz, mono. The moment any app records from the earbud mic, the earbuds switch to headset mode, and your music and videos start sounding like an old phone line.

Fix it by hand

  1. Before the call, open System Settings → Sound → Input and choose the Mac's own microphone (for example "MacBook Air Microphone").
  2. In your call app's audio settings, choose the same microphone if it lets you.
  3. Keep the earbuds as the output. They stay in stereo, because nothing is using their mic.

The trade-off: the other person hears you through the Mac's microphone, so speak towards the laptop.

Make it automatic with CallGuard

Turn on Hi-Fi Buds in CallGuard. Whenever a Bluetooth mic becomes the input, it switches the input back to the Mac's built-in mic. In a live WhatsApp test, WhatsApp followed the change mid-call and the earbuds went from 16 kHz to 44.1 kHz stereo.

If you fall asleep on calls, pair it with Mic Lock, so nobody hears you whichever mic is in use.

Quick answers

Why does Spotify sound bad when I'm on a call with earbuds?

Because the earbuds have switched to Bluetooth headset mode to use their microphone, which is low-quality mono. Using the Mac's microphone instead keeps them in stereo.

Can I use the earbud mic and still have hi-fi sound?

Not with ordinary Bluetooth headset mode: using the earbud mic is what lowers the quality.

Will the other person hear me worse with the Mac mic?

They hear the laptop's microphone instead of the earbud one, so sound is best when you're near the Mac.
