How to stop AirPods or earbuds unmuting you on a call

Updated 16 November 2026 · by notshevvv, maker of CallGuard

Earbud taps can toggle the call app's mute button, so an accidental tap (rolling over in your sleep, for example) unmutes you. Change the tap action in your earbuds' settings, and for a guarantee, lock the microphone at the hardware level with CallGuard's Mic Lock. Apps then receive digital silence whatever their own mute button says.

Why a tap unmutes you

In headset mode, an earbud tap is sent to the call app as a mute or unmute command. The app's own mute button is only a switch inside that app. Anything that flips it, a tap included, puts you back on the call.

Step 1: change the tap action

For AirPods, open System Settings and click your AirPods while they're connected to see their controls. For other brands, use the earbuds' companion app (for example, the maker's phone app). Set the tap you hit in your sleep to "None" or play/pause.

Step 2: lock the microphone itself

CallGuard's Mic Lock mutes every microphone at the hardware level, so every app receives digital silence. In testing, a locked mic recorded 0 non-zero samples. Many "mute" tools only set the input volume to zero, which in our test still let sound through at about −64 dBFS. If anything un-mutes a locked mic, CallGuard mutes it again within milliseconds and counts it.

  • One click on the red LOCKED menu bar icon unmutes you.
  • Hold Control + Option + T to talk while locked. Control + Option + M locks and unlocks from anywhere.
  • It can lock itself when you go quiet on a call, and unlock when the call ends.

macOS doesn't let one app block another app's microphone, so while Mic Lock is on, no app can hear you. That's what makes it reliable.

Quick answers

Can a Mac app mute the microphone for only WhatsApp?

No. macOS doesn't allow one app to block another app's microphone without resetting privacy permissions. Mic Lock mutes the microphones themselves, which covers every app.

Is the mic really off when locked?

Yes. A locked mic delivered exact digital silence in testing (0 non-zero samples), and CallGuard re-mutes it if anything unmutes it.

How do I talk again quickly?

Click the red LOCKED icon in the menu bar, or hold Control + Option + T to talk while it stays locked.
