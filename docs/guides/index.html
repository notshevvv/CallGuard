Guides

Fixes for Mac audio on long calls, in plain English. Updated 16 November 2026.

  • How to stop your Mac lowering music during calls
    Why macOS turns YouTube and Spotify down when a call starts (audio ducking), why there's no setting for it, and how to keep media at full volume on WhatsApp and other calls.
  • Why Bluetooth earbuds sound muffled on Mac calls, and how to fix it
    On a call, Bluetooth earbuds switch to a low-quality headset mode (16 kHz mono). Here's why, how to keep stereo hi-fi by using the Mac's microphone, and how to make it automatic.
  • How to stop AirPods or earbuds unmuting you on a call
    Earbud taps can toggle a call's mute button, often while you sleep. How to stop it: change the tap action, and lock the Mac's microphone at the hardware level so nothing can unmute you.
  • 8D audio and slowed + reverb for any app on Mac
    How to play any song in 8D or slowed + reverb on a Mac, live: Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music and more, with a free menu bar app. Plus Studio Master bass for small earbuds.
